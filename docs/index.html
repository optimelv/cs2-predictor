04 / The read

One series, two moments.

A match before the veto and the same match after official map picks are different prediction problems. StrikeSignal treats them that way.

Before veto--

Who is stronger today?

Team power, player level, roster continuity, recent form, opponent quality, event tier, and series format.

After veto--

Who is stronger on these maps?

Official picks and bans, permabans, map order, side tendencies, map-specific evidence, and the remaining matchup.

Calibration rule

No 100% calls. Sparse teams, roster changes, and uncertain maps pull forecasts toward the middle instead of producing false certainty.
